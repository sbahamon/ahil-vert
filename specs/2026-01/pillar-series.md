# YIMBY Action Pillar Series

5-part video series introducing AHIL and the YIMBY Action pillars.

## Series Overview

- **Total videos**: 5
- **Duration each**: 60 seconds
- **Format**: Numbered series (Part X of 5)
- **Visual style**: Bold text + icons
- **Tone**: Welcoming/inviting
- **Audio**: Text only (music added via TikTok post-render)
- **CTA**: Series tease ("Follow for more"), final video: "Join us"

## Video Structure (each)

```
0-5s:   AHIL logo animation + "Part X of 5" badge
5-15s:  Hook text (bold, attention-grabbing)
15-45s: Pillar explanation (3-4 key points with icons)
45-55s: CTA card
55-60s: Logo + social handle
```
