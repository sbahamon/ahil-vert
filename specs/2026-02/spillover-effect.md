# NWI Spillover Effect

## Type
Standalone Explainer

## Duration
45 seconds (1350 frames @ 30fps)

## Platforms
TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

## Concept
How NIMBYism in Chicago's collar counties doesn't eliminate housing demand — it displaces it into Northwest Indiana (Gary, Hammond, East Chicago), driving up rents and home values in previously affordable communities. Features a stylized animated map showing the spillover.

## Inspiration
Community perspective: collar county NIMBYism has tripled market rents and home values in NWI over the past 5 years. People who used to buy south of I-80/94 are now forced into NWI communities, pricing out existing residents.

## Structure

### Splash (0-3s)
> "Where does housing demand **go?**"

### Hook (3-9s)
> **"It doesn't disappear."**
> Collar counties block new homes.
> NIMBYism shuts the door.

### Stakes (9-15s)
> *Here's the thing:*
> People still need homes.
> Families still need to live **somewhere**.

### Map Animation (15-30s)
> **"So demand moves outward"**
> [Animated SVG map: collar counties show lock barriers, arrows push toward NWI]
> "Northwest Indiana feels the pressure"

### Impact (30-36s)
> **"The result?"**
> - 📈 Rents rise in Gary and Hammond
> - 💰 Home values spike in East Chicago
> - 🚫 Affordability vanishes — there too

### CTA (36-45s)
> Blocking homes in one place
> doesn't solve anything.
> **It just moves the problem.**
> @abundanthousingillinois

## Key Messages
- Blocking homes doesn't eliminate demand — it displaces it
- Collar county NIMBYism has regional consequences
- NWI communities bear the cost of suburban exclusion
- Housing policy is regional, not local
